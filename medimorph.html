MediMorph

Dose conversions made easy. Free for iPhone and iPad.

Get it on the App Store ↗ Contact & support

About

MediMorph converts doses between common opioids, so equivalent doses can be worked out quickly and the math can be checked. Conversions are based on McPherson's Demystifying Opioid Conversion Calculations: A Guide for Effective Dosing.

  • Conversions between common opioids, by oral (PO) and IV/SQ routes where applicable
  • An optional reduced-dose calculation, since the literature suggests reducing the initial dose by 25–50% when switching opioids
  • Each step of the conversion shown alongside the result
  • A history of past calculations, stored on your device
  • Notes, references, and the full disclaimer built in

MediMorph is for educational purposes only. Conversions are approximate and are not a substitute for clinical judgment.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 17.0 or later.
